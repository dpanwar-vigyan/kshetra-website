AskMyBank.ai
AI-powered banking document assistant. Ask questions in plain English about 1,000 customer documents — disputes, complaints, eStatements, and maintenance cases. Dual-engine routing: ClickHouse NL→SQL for aggregations, ChromaDB RAG for content. Built on AWS Bedrock with Nova Lite and Titan Embeddings.

ScanEat
AI-powered food ingredient scanner for iOS & Android. Point your camera at any food label — AWS Textract extracts the ingredient list, Amazon Bedrock (Nova Micro) analyses every ingredient for health impacts with SAFE / CAUTION / AVOID ratings and citations from WHO, FDA, Mayo Clinic, NIH & EFSA. Barcode scanning via OpenFoodFacts. Two-level DynamoDB cache means repeat scans are instant.
AWS Bedrock AWS Textract OCR Expo · React Native OpenFoodFacts DynamoDB Cache
